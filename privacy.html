Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Brain Storm Global Chess Academy respects the privacy of students, parents and visitors. This policy explains how information may be collected and used when you contact the academy, register for coaching, or use academy portals.

Information we may collect

Depending on the service, this may include a student's name, age, parent/guardian contact details, login information, class schedule, attendance, fee records, learning progress and communications with the academy.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide coaching, manage classes, maintain attendance and fee records, communicate schedules and reminders, provide account access, and improve academy services.

Children and student information

Student information should be provided by a parent or legal guardian where required. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to operate the academy.

Photos and recognition

Student photographs, names or achievements may be displayed for recognition only where appropriate permission has been obtained.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect account and student information. Do not share passwords.

Third-party services

The academy website may use services such as website hosting, authentication, databases, messaging or payment providers. Those providers may process information under their own policies.

Contact
